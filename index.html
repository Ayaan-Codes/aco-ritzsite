[ BEFORE WE BEGIN ]

A few things
you might ask.

Do you work on homes and commercial spaces?+

Yes. The Ritz project collection includes residential, workplace, retail, showroom and pharmacy interiors.

Can you help with design and fit-out?+

Interior design and fit-out are both part of our work. The scope for your space is discussed at the start of the project.

What should I prepare for the first conversation?+

Share the location, type of space, any available plans and what you want to achieve. Reference images and an outline of your priorities are useful too.

How are budget and timing decided?+

These depend on the space, scope, materials and site requirements. They need to be discussed for your project rather than estimated from photographs alone.

Can I see more of a particular project?+

Open a project in the collection to explore its image gallery. You can then use the enquiry preview to outline a similar space.
